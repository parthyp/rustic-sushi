Our story

Sushi at the Gateway to the Grand Canyon

For years, a sushi night in Williams meant a drive to Flagstaff. Rustic 66 changes that, bringing a proper sushi bar to the steakhouses and diners of downtown Route 66.

The room is casual and wood-warm, the kind of place where a family off the Grand Canyon Railway, a couple on date night and a table of locals all feel at home. Sushi fans get fresh rolls, nigiri, sashimi and poke; everyone else gets burgers, steaks, salads and a kids’ menu.
